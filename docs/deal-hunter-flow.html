Setup Checklist

1. API Credentials (n8n)

  • Keepa — Free tier: 250 tokens/day (~25 items). Paid plan needed to cover all 47 daily.
  • SerpAPI — 100 free searches/month. ~$50/mo for daily use at this volume.
  • Airtable — Personal access token. Create two tables: Price History and Confirmed Deals.
  • Google Sheets — OAuth2 credential in n8n, pointing to your Approved Products sheet.

2. Approved Products Sheet (populate once, maintain periodically)

Seed from confirmed Practical Sailor Best Choice designations by category — PFDs: Mustang, Spinlock · Anchors: Rocna, Mantus · Tethers: Spinlock · EPIRBs: ACR, Ocean Signal · VHF: Standard Horizon, Icom · Foul weather: Gill, Musto, Henri Lloyd · Fenders: Polyform · Rope: New England Ropes, Samson. Expand from the Practical Sailor gear lab archive at practical-sailor.com.

3. Airtable Schema

  • Price History — item_id (text), date (date), price (currency), retailer (text), source (text)
  • Confirmed Deals — item_id, item_name, brand, current_price, twelve_mo_low, pct_saving, retailer_url, approval_source, logged_at

4. Cold-Start Note

  • The 12-month gate becomes reliable after ~30 days of daily runs. Keepa covers Amazon from day one. For West Marine and Defender prices, manually seed 3–4 months of data for critical safety items using order history or Wayback Machine archived pages. Until then, the gate fires conservatively on Amazon data only.
